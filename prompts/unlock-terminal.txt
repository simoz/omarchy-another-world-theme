Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: compact isolated emblem for the Another World Omarchy theme boot and disk-unlock screen.

Strong clear silhouette, carefully faceted geometry and minimal detail that remains readable at 300 pixels tall.

Style: Refined cinematic low-poly illustration, not photorealistic, not a glossy gaming PC, not an application icon in a square container.
Constraints: No text, wordmark, numbers, watermark, border, extra emblems or logos. This is one isolated transparent-ready artwork, not a screenshot of a login screen.
